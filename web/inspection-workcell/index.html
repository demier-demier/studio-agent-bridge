DETAIL AT THE CONNECTION

Follow the route.

The revision adds cable geometry for two cameras, two illuminators, the HMI and the cabinet supply. New glands meet existing device or cabinet faces; the supply lead terminates at an existing laboratory socket.

Editable Blender splines retain their actual centreline endpoints. Independent readback checks those points against the evaluated gland geometry and attachment faces. Connector names and metadata alone do not establish the connection.

Clips, boots, panel fittings and a floor cover make the proposed routing visible. These are original synthetic design details. Electrical continuity, current capacity, physical bend limits and safety compliance have not been simulated or certified.

What the evidence does not establish

  • Endpoint contact is a geometric observation. It does not establish electrical continuity, mechanical fitness or safety approval.
  • Preservation checks cover the declared mesh and material fields; they do not prove complete Blender-file equivalence.
  • Native images show each renderer's output. They do not establish identical Blender and Unreal appearance.
  • The browser GLB uses browser lighting. Editable cable curves are converted to surface geometry for interchange.
  • Human visual acceptance and engineering approval are separate from automated checks.
